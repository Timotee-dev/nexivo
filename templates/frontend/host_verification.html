Identity Verification

NIN verification is required before you can sell paid tickets.

Submit Your NIN

Your National Identification Number (NIN) is the 11-digit number on your NIN slip or NIMC card. It will be verified against the NIMC database and stored encrypted.

Your NIN is stored encrypted and never shared with third parties.

Why is NIN required?

Nigerian regulations require event platforms to verify the identity of hosts who collect money from ticket sales. NIN verification protects your customers and ensures Nexivo complies with CBN and NITDA guidelines. Free events do not require NIN verification.
